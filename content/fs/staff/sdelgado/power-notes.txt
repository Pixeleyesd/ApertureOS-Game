If central has to be taken down by hand and the software will not do it:

  1. Breaker 7. It is in the ceiling of the annex, above the printer.
     You need the long pole. The long pole is in Imogen's cupboard.
  2. Pull it down and hold it. Central loses its own power in about
     40 seconds.
  3. Write down the time. Say it out loud so someone else hears it.

WARNING: breaker 7 only drops central. It does NOT touch the emitter
bus (the gas supply, separate power, sealed). That line stays live
whatever you do to central. If you cut her off you have cut off her
eyes, not her hands.
