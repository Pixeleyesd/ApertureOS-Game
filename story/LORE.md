# lore notes

What I checked against the Portal wikis (Portal Wiki, Combine OverWiki, the Half-Life wiki) and what the story does with it. If a wiki and the comic disagree I went with the Lab Rat comic.

## things the story relies on

- **Morality core is the only one that stops the neurotoxin.** The others (curiosity, cake/knowledge, anger) just change her personality. In the story the review panel thinks all five are the safety case. Radek and Brandt know it's only MC-01.
- **Chell was number 1498 and Doug moved her to number 1** (Lab Rat, page 21). So the queue has to be longer than 1,498. Ours is 1,603. Her file says "tenacity abnormal, do not test, never gives up".
- **Staff get forced into testing after the flood** and Chell is the last one standing. So the story has her testing staff first, then the queue, and Chell only moves up at the very end.
- **Doug is the only survivor of the flood** and hides in places she can't watch.
- In the comic the Morality core is shown *before* the flood, and the flood only happens after Henry approves "a little neurotoxin" for a science experiment. That's why every question she asks in chapter 5 gets granted either way. Later chapters can reuse that for the gas request.

## things I'm not sure of (decide when you get there)

- **Spelling.** Every wiki spells it **Rattmann** (two n's). The game says Rattman and the login is `drattman`. Easy to change, it's just a find and replace.
- **Date.** The wikis say the flood happened in May 200-, on the first Bring Your Daughter to Work Day, in the middle of a normal working day. The game is a March 2011 night shift. You picked 2011 so I left it, but if you want the night to be the eve of that day, say so.
- **Intelligence core.** In the games the "Intelligence" core and the cake core are usually the same thing (the Knowledge core that recites a cake recipe). Wheatley is a different thing, the Intelligence *Dampening* core, built to make her dumber. The game has both intelligence and cake as separate cores, so I made chapter 6 the dampening core.
- **Doug himself.** In the comic he's a scientist/programmer who works on the portal gun, takes antipsychotics ("for the end times") and has a Companion Cube he talks to. The game has him as a night technician. Nothing wrong with that, but you could plant the pills somewhere in his home folder.
- **Henry.** He's the scientist in Lab Rat who shows Doug the Morality core and approves the neurotoxin. Brandt is doing a similar job in the game. Could be the same person with a different name, or a new one.
- **Version number.** The boot log says GLaDOS v1.07. The wikis say 1.07 was from an old ARG image and the official one is 3.11. Doesn't matter much.
- **Brenda.** One of the names on the dead-employee list in Lab Rat is Brenda Bogenschutz. The mop in the game is called Brenda. It works as a nod.
