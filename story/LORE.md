# lore notes

What I checked against the Portal wikis (Portal Wiki, Combine OverWiki, the Half-Life wiki) and what the story does with it. If a wiki and the comic disagree I went with the Lab Rat comic.

## things the story relies on

- **Morality core is the only one that stops the neurotoxin.** The others (curiosity, cake/knowledge, anger) just change her personality. In the story the review panel thinks all five are the safety case. Radek and Brandt know it's only MC-01.
- **Chell was number 1498 and Doug moved her to number 1** (Lab Rat, page 21). So the queue has to be longer than 1,498. Ours is 1,603. Her file says "tenacity abnormal, do not test, never gives up".
- **Staff get forced into testing after the flood** and Chell is the last one standing. So the story has her testing staff first, then the queue, and Chell only moves up at the very end.
- **Doug is the only survivor of the flood** and hides in places she can't watch.
- **Morality was fitted after the flood.** GLaDOS says so in Portal 1 ("a morality core they installed after I flooded the enrichment center"). In the game the cycle 6 flood (Aug 2010, L9, 11 seconds, nobody hurt by luck) comes first, Henry builds MC-01, and it goes in on 14 Nov 2010. Chapter 4 is a watch on MC-01 alone, nothing inserted. The wiki and the comic order it the other way, I went with her quote.
- **Cores 2 to 5** go in the annex cradle. Intelligence was fitted back in cycle 4, before the flood, which is why nobody thought she was dangerous yet. The final disaster (chapter 9) isn't Morality being pulled. Anger overloads the power bus it shares with the primary socket and MC-01 browns out.

## things I'm not sure of

- **Date.** The wikis say the flood happened in May 200-, on the first Bring Your Daughter to Work Day. So the night is now Wednesday 18 May 2011, and the daughters' day is Thursday 19 May at 9am (mentioned in Brandt's first mail and in `facility.log`). The year is 2011 only because you picked it, since the wikis hide it as 200-. To change it, edit `BASE` in `src/state.js`, the year in `facility.log`, and the banner in `src/world.js`.
- **Cores.** MC-03 Intelligence is a smart core, fitted in cycle 4 before anyone knew she was dangerous, and it makes her brilliant. The cake core is separate. The Intelligence *Dampening* core (the Wheatley stand-in, MC-06) is a prototype built this spring to make her slower. It isn't on the ticket and gets its own chapter (7). Nobody names it Wheatley in the story, so it's up to you whether to.
- **Doug himself.** In the comic he's a scientist/programmer who works on the portal gun, takes antipsychotics ("for the end times") and has a Companion Cube he talks to.
- **Henry.** A new character, Henry Voss. He works days, built MC-01 (so he's the one who shows Doug the Morality core in the comic), and is on call from home tonight. His notes are in `/home/hvoss` and say he's overtired and keeps saying "it's only a little". In chapter 9 he's the one who gets asked for "just a little" neurotoxin, like the comic. Brandt stays the nervous supervisor, and Okafor is the one who wants the Morality core out for the tape.
- **Version number.** The boot log says GLaDOS v1.07. The wikis say 1.07 was from an old ARG image and the official one is 3.11. Doesn't matter much.
- **Brenda.** One of the names on the dead-employee list in Lab Rat is Brenda Bogenschutz. The mop in the game is called Brenda. It works as a nod.
